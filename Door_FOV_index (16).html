Sensor Setup

Position (door-local Y,Z, origin = hinge) and yaw are fixed to the door — the sensor lifts and rotates with it. Yaw is the sensing direction in the Y–Z plane, measured CCW: 0 = +Y, 90 = up (+Z), 180 = −Y, 270 = down. For a LH door, outboard-down is around 195–200°.

Obstacle Setup

Obstacle positions are in the vehicle frame; the front view uses Y (horizontal) and Z (height). X is fore-aft.

Static FOV Test Points

Enable “Add point on click”, then click the canvas — a test point is created at the current door position, and its in-FOV status (PASS/FAIL) is computed automatically. Points can also be added manually.

Map Regions (Top View)

Draw measured-FOV / coverage areas on the Top (X–Y) view. Click + Add region, then click the top view to drop corner points; double-click or Finish to close it. Use Move to drag the whole area as a group, or Edit to drag corners, drag an edge to insert a corner, or right-click a corner to delete it. Each region shows its enclosed area (m²).

Coverage Zones

Zones are fixed bands in the Front (Y–Z) plane — they stay put while the door lifts. Coverage = the share of a zone that falls inside any active sensor's FOV. Because the sensors ride the door, it is measured at every door position; Worst (the smallest across all positions) is the safety-relevant figure. Turn on Coverage zones in the toolbar overlays to see covered (green) vs gap (red) samples.

Distances are outboard from the vehicle side (mm); heights are above ground (mm).

Compare Layouts

Load two saved project JSON files that differ only in their sensors (positions / angles). The door geometry must match — the comparison is rejected otherwise, since the coverage numbers would not be comparable. Zones and grid step are taken from the current project so both sides are scored identically.
